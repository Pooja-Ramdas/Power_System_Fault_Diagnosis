As reported by Transelec S.A.: “At 03:20 hours on December 19, 2024, there was a forced disconnection of line 66 kV Charrua - Laja, due to a one-phase ground failure in phase A as a result of the cutting and theft of drivers between structures 6 and 7. Following the repair and work work, the line is in service at 09:44 hours on December 19, 2024.”
