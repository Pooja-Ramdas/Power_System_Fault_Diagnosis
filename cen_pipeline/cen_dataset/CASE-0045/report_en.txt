Status and configuration prior to the failure The transmission facilities were in normal service at the pre-forced moments, with the following peculiarities: 6640) for the commissioning of the project NUP 3259 “Enlargement S/E Chiguayante”, the bars N°1 and N°2 15 kV of S/E Chiguayante were coupled (closed 89CS) and supplied from the cloth C3 of S/E Army, through a gill between the feeders Las Violetas (paño C6) of S/E Chiguayante and Chepe (paño C3) of S/E Army.
