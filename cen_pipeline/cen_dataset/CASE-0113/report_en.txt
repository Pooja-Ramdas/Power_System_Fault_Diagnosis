Other relevant background According to the information of the company Chilquinta Transmitación S.A.: “Before the event happened on Monday 30 September 2024 in the Feeder Llolleo, it is important to specify that the switch 52CB2A is a maneuvering equipment (without arc extension chamber), which has the ability to follow the position of the switch 52C4. In addition, the relay GE 12IAC53 when operating, emits a signal that is wired to the control Form 6, causing an “ext trip” triggering the opening of the switch 52C4, and consequently, the switch 52CBC2A remains open. On the other hand, the current of imbalance (3I0) that generates the operation of the relé GE 12IAC53, has its origin in an element constitutive of the bank of condenser 2A of S/E San Antonio. It is important to note, that it finds technological obsolescence of the protection equipment and the control scheme associated to the bank in contact, Chilquinta S. for new ones that allow the registration of events and oscillography, having as estimated date of commission Julio of 2025”. Depending on the history presented to the date of issue of this EAF, the following information will be requested from the company Chilquinta S.A.: • Schedule of activities, with defined dates, to carry out the replacement of the protection relays associated to the bank of condenser BC2A of S/E San Antonio, by equipment that allow to have fault records. • Considering the determination of the proposed physical phenomenon as “Origin not determined”, the delivery of results of the analyses after the investigation of the opening of the switch 52C4 of S/E San Antonio, that allow to elucidate the possible cause of its origin, together with their respective backups and possible corrective measures to avoid its repetition.
