As indicated by Transelec S.A.: “At 00:16 hours on August 2, 2024, there was the forced disconnection of the line 154 kV Sauzal - Rancagua, owned by Transelec S.A., due to the operation of its protections. This, because of a monophase short circuit to ground in phase B with evolution to biphasic fault between phases B and C to ground. At 01:17 hours on August 02, 2024, Transelec personnel performed normalization of service maneuvers, closing switch 52A3 S/E Sauzal, leaving in service and energizing the line 154 kV Sauzal - Rancagua.
