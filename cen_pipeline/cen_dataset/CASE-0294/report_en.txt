A forced B3 panel of the S/E Charrua, associated with the Charrua transmission line – Laja 66 kV, owned by Transelec S.A., due to a single phase fault to ground in phase B. Subsequently, an attempt to manually reconnect test is made at 02:25 hours on the same day with success. However, only two phases are detected, so that the Line remains radial from S/E Charrua, opening the 52B2 switch of S/E Laja. At 03:09 hours, a second disconnection occurs due to a single phase fault to ground in phase A, while at 03:12 hours manual reconnection maneuver authorized by the National Electrical Coordinator, without success, so that the line remains in permanent failure. The cause of the fault was the theft of driver, with damage caused at four points other than the Line; between the structures E-8 to E-14 with cutting of the three phases in vain of E-13 and damage to the structure E-12, between structures E-134 to E-136 with the structure of the central structure E-1.
