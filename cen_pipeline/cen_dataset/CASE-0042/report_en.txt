As of December 04, 2024, there was the forced disconnection of line 66 kV Linares - Chacahuín, owned by Transelec, due to the operation of its protection systems, resulting from an overload caused by the increase of power transfers by this line. It should be mentioned that prior to the occurrence of the disconnection, the National Electrical Coordinator (CEN) was notified of the existence of a risk of protection operation, product of a high level of load in the cloth B3 of S/E Linares, requesting authorization to normalize immediately in case of the opening of the switch. At the first attempt to close the switch B3 in S/E Linares, there is the opening of the 52B1 switch in S/E Panimávida, product of the systemic conditions associated with an increase in the current and decrease of the tension in the line, presumably due to the loss of synchronism by the rapid manual reconnection.
