Status and configuration prior to the failure The transmission facilities were in normal service at the moments prior to the forced disconnect. Other relevant background As reported by the company Chilquinta Transmission S.A. transmission line 44 [kV] Catemu - Los Angeles circuit N° 1, driven by the residual distance function (21N), affecting the consumption of our only customer Minera Cerro Negro in the substation (S/E) Los Angeles. The Maintenance Area Transmission, is directed to the substation (S/E) Catemu with the fault locator team, to perform the continuity test to the electric conductors of the line, being normal, with mechanical and electrical continuity in its three phases and in all its extension of 20,7 [Km], until the end of the line in the substation (S/E) Los Angeles. At the same time, the patrol of the transmission line 44 [kV] Catemu - Los Angeles circuit N° 1, in a pedestrian way and with support of unmanned aerial vehicle (RPAS), being possible to locate and identify the physical phenomenon causing the electrical failure, characterized by the presence of a bird that reduced the dielectric distance between the energized driver bridge of the left lateral phase and the metallic crossover which gives it support to the traction chain N°. and therefore, the electrical phenomenon corresponding to a single phase over current to ground. Once the inspection and repair of the affected installation is completed, the normal state of all components is verified and the Control Center Transmission of Chilquinta Transmission S.A. is informed that the line is in a position to be energized again. The Control Center Transmission of Chilquinta Transmission S.A., then requests the authorization of the National Electrical Coordinator of the energyization of the line 44 [kV] Catemu - Los Angeles circuit N° 1, which is produced successfully”.
