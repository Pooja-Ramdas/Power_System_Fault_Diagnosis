Other relevant background As reported by Transelec S.A. company: “At 11:18 hours on August 1, there was the disconnection of circuit 1 of line 66 kV Pullinque - Los Lagos, owned by Transelec, by operation of its protections. This, due to a biphasic short circuit between phases B and C, as a result of the strong and unusual climatic conditions existing in the wind and rain zone, producing the fall of tree on the line, which was located outside the safety strip. This disconnection caused the interruption of electricity supply to the consumptions of the S/E Panguipulli, with a power loss of 3.8 MW according to our report from SCADA. Regarding the replenishment of the supply, these are recovered at 00:50 hours on August 2nd with the reconnection of section 66 kV Pullinque - Panguipulli.
