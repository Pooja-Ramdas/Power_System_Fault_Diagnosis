The tree was located outside the security strip, almost 25 30 meters from the axis of the line, within a private land (Avisico Trinidad SA) specifically in the Region of Sangurimito 390a. According to technical specifications RPTD N° 07: resulting 17 meters on each side of the axis of the line.
