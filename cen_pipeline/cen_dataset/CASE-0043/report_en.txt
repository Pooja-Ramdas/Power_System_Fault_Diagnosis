Description of the configurations at the moments before and after the failure Pre-failure system demand: 8889.16 MW Frequency Regulation Distributed Frequency Control in the National Electrical System, prior to failure, by means of the plants El Toro (U1, U2, U3 and U4), Mujillones (CTM3 and IEM), Ralco (ex and U2) and Rapel. s 05:35 hours on Tuesday, December 03, 2024, occurred the untimely operation of the switch 52ET1 of S/E Las Canteras, driven by the function of the over current defined function programmed in the relay mark EFACEC model TPU T500 associated to the power transformer 110/23 kV N°1, affecting momentarily the consumptions of the feeders 23 kV, Data Center 1, Data Center 2, Data Center 3 and Data Center 4. The transmission maintenance area, when carrying out the on-the-spot inspection of the affected equipment, was able to locate and identify the physical phenomenon causing the electrical indisponibility, which corresponded to a domestic feline (cat) that climbed over the power switch 52E1, shortening the dielectric distance between the energy points of the switch bushing with the supporting metal structure of these equipment, resulting in the respective triphasic short circuit. The above, provokes a fulgor of energy that pollutes the insulation of the switch 52E1 and the date disconnector is reported 89E1-2. • Clarification of the specific fault point in the S/E Las Canteras 52E1 switch structure, i.e. the bar side (between the 89E1-1 disconnector and the bushings on the source side of the switch) or the line side (between the 89E1-2 disconnector and the bushings on the load side of the switch), for the purpose of identifying the TTCCs that detected the failure and justifying the reason for not having operated the overcurrent protection associated with the E1 cloth instead of that associated with the ET1 cloth. • Sending the oscillographic records of the E1 panel relay of S/E Las Canteras in COMTRADE format, and sending their event records in text format and COMTRADE format, together with the corresponding analysis based on their adjustments. • Comunas affected by the interruption of the supply in each feeder. In addition, the reports of failure of installations entered into the National Coordinator's system are attached to the previous Edjectorneex Chile V SpA. (b) Short-term corrective actions: The company Edgeconnex Chile V SpA indicates the following corrective actions: “As an immediate corrective measure, the insulation of the affected equipment is cleaned and isolation tests are carried out, verifying that the equipment can be energized again. As a means of collection, the total isolation of the substation was revised without finding obvious damage in other equipment of S/E Las Canteras. As a mitigation measure, insulating tape is installed in the connectors of the 52E1 switch to reduce the risk of short-circuits”. c) Long-term corrective actions: The company Edgeconnex Chile SpA does not indicate corrective actions.
