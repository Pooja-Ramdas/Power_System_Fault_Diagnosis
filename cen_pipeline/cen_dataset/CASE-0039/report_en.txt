Other relevant background As reported by the company Chilquinta Transmission S.A.: “At 16:10 hours on Saturday, December 7, 2024, there was the forced disconnection of the transmission line 44 [kV] Catemu - Los Angeles Circuit No. 1, operated by the distance function (21), affecting the consumption of our only client Minera Cerro Negro in the substation (S/E) Los Angeles. The Maintenance Area Transmission had begun patrolling of the transmission line 44 [kV] Catemu - Los Angeles Circuit No. 1, in a pedestre way being possible to locate and identify the physical phenomenon causing the electrical failure, which was characterized by a forest fire of grasslands, and native shrubs, spreading under the electric conductors of the hollow between structures No. 57 to No. 59 of the mentada line, with the extreme heat under the structure N°59 that caused burst the genores of support medium-grade porcelain, , the electrical phenomenon corresponding to a biphasic current. Once the inspection and repair of the affected installation has been completed, maintenance personnel are directed to the substation (S/E) Catemu with the fault locator equipment, to perform the continuity test to the electric conductors of the line, being normal, with mechanical and electrical continuity in its three phases and in its entire extension of 20.7 [Km], until the end of the line in the substation (S/E) Los Angeles. Since the normal state of all the components of the installation has been repaired and verified, the Control Center Transmission of Chilquinta Transmission S.A., is informed that the line is in condition to be energized again. The Control Center Transmission of Chilquinta Transmission S.A., then requests the authorization of the National Electrical Coordinator of the energization of the line 44 [kV] Catemu - Los Angeles Circuit N° 1, which is produced in a successful way.”.
