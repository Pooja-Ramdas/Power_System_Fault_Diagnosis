Other relevant background As reported by STM S.A.: “On Wednesday, November 6, 2024 at 17:46, the MT1 switch (52CT1) was opened unexpectedly during the process of testing the protections of the transformer N°4 of the Lord Cochrane substation, this on the occasion of the Audit of Protections of this substation. Specifically during the tests to the protections of the transformer N°4, at the time of sending the shot to the 52CT2 switch, the 52CT2 switch was opened. The 52CT1 and MT1 bars were left without supply. This was due to an incorrect wire that had the firing lines (as detected in subsequent research). It should be noted that this activity was being carried out by enabled personnel and with the work permit 2024-11435.”
