This protection operated after a fault in the distribution network of the feeder Portezuelo was detected by its protection, but was not cleared in due time, because the switch of head of that feeder experienced a destructive failure in arc extinction chamber (white phase). The arc extinction medium of these chambers is through the gas SF6, so presumably the partial leak or degradation of this dielectric gas caused the current of failure not to be extinguished in this phase and that current of failure ended evolving to a destructive failure of the switch.
