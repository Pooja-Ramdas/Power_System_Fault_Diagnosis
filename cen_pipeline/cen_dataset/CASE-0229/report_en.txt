Other relevant background As reported by the Metropolitan Transmitter Society S.A.: “On Friday August 2, 2024 at 00:53 hours, there was the automatic operation (OA) of the 52ET3 switch (MT to Main Bar MT3 (MT3P) switch) of S/E Lo Boza. This because the switch of the Waters Claras feeder (E19), did not open upon receiving the order to open its protection. According to the pre-event records, the protection of the Waters Claras feeder presented multiple pickups due to faults in its MT network, caused by bad weather affecting the metropolitan region. During the normalization maneuvers carried out by the control center to resupply the service, attempts were made to normalize the MT3P bar again (02:30 hrs and 02:54 hrs.) with all its feeders.
