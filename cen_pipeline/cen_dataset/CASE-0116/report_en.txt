Status and configuration prior to failure The transmission facilities were in normal service at the moments prior to forced disconnection, with the following particularities: - The units N°1 and N°2 of central Chapiquiña were in service with a generation of 2 MW c/u. - Due to the schedule of the event, the FPV Pampa Camarones, which connects to the SEN via the line 23 kV Chaca - Tap Off Vitor Mobile N°2, was in service without primary resource for power generation. - The S/E Tap Off Quiani 52B1 switch, associated with the line 66 kV Tap Off Quiani - Parinacota, was open (normal condition of operation). Other relevant background provided by the company Engie Energe Chile S.A.: “Being the 00:29 Hrs., there is intempensive disconnection of the line 110 kV there is Arica - Pozo Almonte by operation of protection 2 units. line 110 kV, found Single phase Transformer of Mal Pass in failure, after this electrical tests are carried out detecting loss of the quality of internal isolation of the transformer”. Other background provided by the Coordinator: According to SCADA records of the Coordinator, prior to the failure in the single phase transformer of S/E Tap Off Mal Paso, the transfer by the HT3 cloth of S/E Arica was less than 1 MW, which allowed that, after the forced disconnection of the line 110 kV Arica - Pozo Almonte, the SS/EE Arica, Central Diesel Arica and Chapichena were kept operating on a stable electric island, supplied by units N°1 and N°2 of central Chapiquiña.
