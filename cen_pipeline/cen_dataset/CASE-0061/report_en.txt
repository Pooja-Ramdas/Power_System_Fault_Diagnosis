Status and configuration prior to failure Pre-failure, the transformer N°1 220/23 kV of S/E Los Vilos was out of service by the SDCF N°2024121689 in force to date. Other relevant background As indicated by Minera Los Pelambres in its Reports of Failure 48 hours and 5 days: “Maintenance personnel carrying out cleaning in mechanical relays of T1 transformer (the one that was out of service), activated one of these relays, which originated operation of 52 ET2 on the switch. This abnormality manifests itself in its own protection system, which instead of operating on the same T1 transformer, wrongly affects the T2 transformer's output switch. A cross operation between the mechanical protections of the T1 transformer and the T2 transformer is verified. The correct thing is that this system must operate separately and independently, pointing each protection system at its own transformer.”
