Status and configuration prior to failure The transmission facilities were in normal service at the time of forced disconnection. of December 2024, there was the forced disconnection of the transmission line 110 kV Concón – Reñaca circuit 1, operated by the function of distance of the protection, in particular by zone 1, affecting the consumption of the substation (S/E) Reñaca. The Maintenance Area Transmission had begun the patrolling of the transmission line 110 kV Concón – Reñaca circuit 1, in a manner pedestre and with support of unmanned aerial vehicle (RPAS), being possible to locate and identify the physical phenomenon causing the electrical failure, consisting of the intervention of unauthorized third parties in the installations, who recklessly tried to steal the electrical conductor of the middle phase in the traction structure No 48, generating consequently, the electrical phenomenon corresponding to a monophasic current to ground. Parallel, maintenance personnel goes to the substation (S/E) Concon with the fault locator equipment, to perform the test of continuity to the electric conductors of the line, being normal, with mechanical and electrical continuity in its three phases and in all its extension of 9,3 [line] a in the substation (S/E) Reñaca. Once the inspection has been completed and the normal state of all the components of the installation is verified, the Control Center Transmission of Chilquinta Transmission S.A. is informed that the line is in a position to be energized again. The Control Center Transmission of Chilquinta Transmission S.A., then requests the authorization of the National Electrical Coordinator of the energize of the transmission line 110 kV Concón – Reñaca circuit 1, which is produced successfully.”
