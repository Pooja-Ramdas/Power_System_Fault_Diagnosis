Other relevant background As reported by the South Transmission System S.A.: “On Thursday, August 1, 2024 at 10:20 hours, there is the interruption of the line 66 kV Licanco – Las Violetas, in Licanco Substation opens 52B2 for protection action. This event leaves out of service to the Las Violetas and Nueva Imperial Substations and affects 23,957 customers of the communes of Nueva Imperial, Carahue, Saavedra and Cholchol. According to the data obtained from the protections, the point of failure 27.5 km from SE Licanco is identified. Maintenance personnel leave immediately to look for cause of interruption, where it is found that between the structures 147 to 148 a truck of third parties that in maneuvers of materials under the electrical line makes contact with the lateral failure. 5 we proceed to energize the line from Substation Licanco to Las Violetas, and then energization of the Las Violetas section – Nueva Imperial with normalized consumption.” Frontel S.A. carried out the “normalization of consumptions through maneuvers in Dx or Local Generation”.
