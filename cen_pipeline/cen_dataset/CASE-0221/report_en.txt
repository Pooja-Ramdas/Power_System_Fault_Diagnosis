Description of the configurations at the moments before and after the failure Pre-failure system demand: 7359.0 MW Frequency Regulation Distributed Frequency Control in the National Electrical System prior to failure, using the Mejillones (IM and CTM3) plants, Ralco (U1 and U2) and El Toro (U1, U2 and U4). Status and prior to failure prior to S24 transmission facilities were in normal service. At 01:52 hours the automatic operation of the MT1 switch (52CT1) occurred, due to the performance of its master relay (86B1). The 86B1 action was activated by the overcurrent protection of the MT1 switch, specifically the overcurrent unit of reverse phase time. It should be noted that at that time there was intense temporary wind and rain in Santiago with multiple faults in the distribution network.” Depending on the background presented to the date of issue of this EAF, the following information will be requested from the company Enel Distribución Chile S.A.: • 5th day failure report, with the corresponding level of detail. In September 2024, the MT1 bar switch protection scheme (52CT1) will be reviewed to verify its correct functioning.” c) Long-term corrective actions: The STM S.A. company does not indicate long-term corrective actions.
